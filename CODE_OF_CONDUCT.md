# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Keep discussion about the work, explain technical
disagreements with evidence, and accept corrections. Harassment, discriminatory
remarks, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation
to protect contributors. For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately
through the contact options on [the GitHub profile](https://github.com/mithudso).
Do not post private reports or identifying details in public issues. No response
time is guaranteed.
