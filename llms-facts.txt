- The subcommands are mirror, bulk, render, diff, merge, index, novelty, fetch, clean and extract [src: README.md]
- The bulk subcommand runs a 3-stage offline funnel: exact-hash dedup, optional lexical stage, then semantic embed and cluster into a grouped master .md [src: README.md]

- Every extracted unit carries a source_anchor equal to its originating page URL plus #L<page-local-line> [src: README.md]
- The embedding model defaults to mxbai-embed-large and is set with DISTILL_EMBED_MODEL; the Ollama endpoint is set with OLLAMA_HOST [src: README.md]
- Every Ollama path degrades gracefully to lexical difflib matching when the host is down [src: README.md]
- Outputs land in ~/.claude/distillations/ with vector indexes under .index/ [src: README.md]
