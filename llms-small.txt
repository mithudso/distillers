# distillers (small)

> Offline document-distillation engine, the zero-LLM-token half of a docs-to-skill pipeline. distill_offline.py (stdlib-only Python) mechanically reduces large text corpora, especially web-text-mirror docset mirrors, by splitting, extracting, deduplicating, rendering, indexing and novelty filtering.

## Essentials

- Script: python3 distill_offline.py <subcommand>
- Subcommands: mirror, bulk, render, diff, merge, index, novelty, fetch, clean, extract
- Env: OLLAMA_HOST, DISTILL_EMBED_MODEL (default mxbai-embed-large)
- Verify: python3 -m py_compile distill_offline.py
- Every Ollama path degrades to lexical (difflib) matching

See llms.txt for the index and llms-facts.txt for sourced facts.
